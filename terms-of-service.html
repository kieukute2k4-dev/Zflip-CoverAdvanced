During a mirror session, the bridge may change display size, density, rotation, display power, and a system mirror setting. It saves the pre-session state and attempts to restore it when the session ends. It may separately remember display values you changed while mirroring so they can be used in a later session.

Before clearing app data, uninstalling the app, or revoking Wireless debugging, deactivate the mirror and wait for restoration to finish. Removing the app or its ADB authorization while a session is active can prevent the bridge from completing restoration. If recovery is pending, use the app's recovery action before removing it.
